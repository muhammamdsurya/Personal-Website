Live SaaS · Point of sale

KaselaPOS

Offline-first cashier app for Indonesian small businesses. It keeps selling when the internet drops, then syncs and reports across every branch.

LaravelOffline-first syncMulti-branch reporting
Live site
Role
Founder & full-stack developer
Product
Live SaaS, three paid plans
For
Cafés, restaurants & small retail

The problem

Many small shops in Indonesia run on patchy internet. A typical online POS stops taking orders the moment the connection drops, while a dedicated POS machine is expensive and keeps the numbers locked inside the outlet. Owners often have to visit the store just to see the day's sales.

What I built

  • A browser-based cashier for any phone, tablet or laptop: no install, no special hardware.
  • Offline mode: sales are stored on the device and sync automatically when the connection returns, for 24 to 168 hours depending on the plan.
  • The store's own QRIS on the cashier screen, so payments go straight to the merchant's account.
  • Inventory you can audit: stock in and out, stock opname, waste, full movement history, and recipes that deduct raw materials on every sale.
  • Shift handover with a cash-drawer count and recorded variance, plus consolidated reports across branches.

The interesting part

Offline sales break naive reporting. If a sale made at 9 PM only syncs the next morning, a report keyed on server time puts it on the wrong day. KaselaPOS computes every report from the transaction time recorded on the device, not the sync time, so daily revenue, net profit and branch comparisons stay correct no matter when the data arrives. It's the same event-time vs. processing-time problem every data pipeline has to solve.

Outcome

Live in production with a 14-day free trial and three paid plans (Starter, Growth, Pro), from a single outlet up to ten branches.

Live SaaS · Automation

AutoJobs

Job-application automation across JobStreet, Glints and LinkedIn: one search, explainable matching, queued applications, and a full report.

Next.jsTypeScriptPostgreSQLChrome extension
Live site Source
Role
Founder & full-stack developer
Product
Web app + browser extension
For
Job seekers & career switchers

The problem

Job hunting means doing the same work three times: retyping your profile, re-uploading your CV and answering the same screening questions on JobStreet, Glints and LinkedIn, while sifting through hundreds of listings that don't fit. A few weeks in, nobody remembers where they applied.

What I built

  • One search campaign across three portals, filtered by title, description keywords, experience level and companies to avoid.
  • A mandatory preview that shows why each listing matched, or why it was filtered out, before anything is sent.
  • A Chrome/Edge extension that fills and submits applications in the user's own browser, reusing saved answers across portals.
  • A dry-run mode that completes every form without pressing submit.
  • A report of every attempt with proof of submission and an Excel export.

The interesting part

PostgreSQL doubles as the job queue. Each application is claimed with FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED, so one portal account never runs two attempts at once, and the randomized 120–180 second gap and the 25-per-day cap hold even with several browsers paired. The extension is a thin executor: portal adapters, answer logic, retries and caps all live on the server, so fixing a portal is a server deploy, not an extension update.

The data angle

Underneath, it's an ETL pipeline: extract listings from three sources, normalize and score them against the campaign's criteria with a reason for every decision, then load the chosen ones into a queue and a report.

Built responsibly

AutoJobs never solves CAPTCHAs or 2FA and stores no portal passwords or cookies. When a portal asks for verification, the extension brings its window forward and waits for the user.

Booking platform

NNRooms

Hourly apartment rental in Karawang with live slot availability, 1–24 hour bookings, and online payment.

LaravelPostgreSQLTailwindCSS
Source
Role
Full-stack developer
Product
Short-stay booking web app
For
Property owners & guests

The problem

Short-stay apartments in Karawang rent by the hour, not the night. Handling 1- to 24-hour bookings over chat and spreadsheets invites double bookings, and guests can't see what's actually free.

What I built

  • Search by check-in date and duration: 1, 3, 6, 8, 12 or 24 hours.
  • Room pages with facilities, ratings and guest reviews.
  • A live availability grid that marks every slot as available, booked or under maintenance, with its price.
  • Checkout with full payment or a down payment, by bank virtual account (BCA, Mandiri) or e-wallet (OVO, DANA).

The interesting part

Availability is a data-modeling problem. Every booking and maintenance window is a time range, and a slot can only be sold if it overlaps none of them, across durations from one hour to a full day. Modeling it that way in PostgreSQL is what keeps the availability grid accurate and double bookings out.

Outcome

One place for owners to manage listings and schedules, and a self-serve flow for guests from search to payment.

Booking platform

Basecamp Arena

Sports-venue booking for badminton, padel, tennis, futsal and more, with real-time slots and no sign-up needed.

LaravelMySQLFilament
Source
Role
Full-stack developer
Product
Venue booking + admin panel
For
Venue owners & players

The problem

Sports venues still take bookings by phone and WhatsApp. Owners juggle schedules by hand, and players can't tell which courts are free without asking first.

What I built

  • A catalog of venues (badminton, padel, pickleball, tennis, squash, futsal, golf) with hourly prices.
  • A slot picker that shows every hour as ready, taken, pending, under repair or selected.
  • Guest booking with no account required, confirmed on WhatsApp after payment.
  • A Filament admin panel where owners manage courts, prices and schedules.

The interesting part

Dropping the login removes friction, but it also removes identity. The schedule compensates with explicit slot states: an hour waiting on payment is held as pending instead of being sold twice, and courts under repair simply drop out of the grid.

Outcome

Players book on their own, and owners run a single schedule instead of a chat thread.

The brief

Resi's Project wanted to sell job-focused chemistry and lab-analyst courses online: a storefront for classes and bundles, a protected space for paid material, and tools that help learners land the job afterwards.

What we built

  • A course catalog with single classes and bundles, from inorganic chemistry to internship and career prep.

KotlinREST APIsTechnical Writing
Role
Android developer
Product
Community delivery app
For
Customers, merchants & drivers

The idea

A delivery platform where the community shares in the value it creates. Members invite friends with a referral code and earn when those friends order.

What I built

  • Sign-in with email, phone or Google, with an optional referral code at sign-up.
  • Food ordering from nearby merchants, plus package delivery with pickup and drop-off.
